What we record

When you use LOOP or CHAOS, we record the pages you visit, searches and API requests, and CHAOS-Agent conversations, including the questions, responses, and database queries. These records include your account, time of access, IP address, and browser.

We use this information to operate and improve the tools and to report aggregate usage. Reports contain summary statistics, such as the number of users by country, institution type, research area, or career stage, not individual activity.

Usage records are retained for the life of the projects plus five years.
